# lapG7, the fair laptop: set-up (2026-10-17)

lapG7 (192.168.1.66 on the home Wi-Fi) is the dev host at the fair and a fallback brain: the
hat by voice only, never motors. It is the June laptop fish. Commit bf6b7fd (`sorting-hat`).
The set-up was run over ssh from the desktop.

`sorting-hat`. Its June `.env` is parked in `~/.fish_env_june/`, outside the repo.

## The laptop's `.env` (names; values not shown)

- From the board's `.env`: the two keys and `FISH_PTT_KEY`, `FISH_PTT_MODE`, `FISH_PTT_MAX_S`,
  `FISH_READING_LOOK`, `FISH_STT_KEYWORDS`, `FISH_WELCOME_ONCE`, `FISH_BURBLE`,
  `FISH_FILLER_DELAY_S`.
- Added for the laptop:
  - the hat: `FISH_PERSONA=hat`, `FISH_MOUTH_MODE=gate`, `FISH_LLM_PROFILE=luna`,
    `FISH_VOICE_QUIT=false`;
  - the voice: `FISH_TTS_BACKEND=loq`, `FISH_LOQ_BIN`, `LOQ_LTTS`, `FISH_STT_BACKEND=openai`;
  - no motors: `FISH_SERIAL_PORT=/dev/null-no-motors`, `FISH_SERIAL_REQUIRED=false`;
  - the page: `FISH_VIZ_HOST=0.0.0.0`, for his own hotspot only.
