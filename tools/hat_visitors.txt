# Scripted visitors for tools/hat_rehearse.py, for the adult audience (2026-10-27): his own
# visit of today, a couple at a party, a sceptic, a costume, one-word answers, a sad one, a
# rude one, "¿eres un robot?", one at the house, a badge; each with a fortune. A blank line
# starts a new visitor. Cue values today's code refuses (pareja, escéptico, fiestero, joven,
# mayor, disfraz) are reported by the rehearsal and not sent.

# Since ADR 0026 (2026-10-29) the animal is gone: each fortune has a wish (deseo) and the card
# is a yes (the hat draws), a card named (the visitor's deck) or a no.
